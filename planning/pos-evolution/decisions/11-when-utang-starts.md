## Decision

- Utang is booked **at release**, for exactly what left the store (decision 5 unchanged). Accepting books nothing.
- An accepted request exposes an **expected amount** (store prices × accepted quantities) that CPMS may read for budgeting. It is not a debt and is not in receivables.

## Why

Booking on accept would need refunds or corrections every time a release is short or the remainder is closed.
