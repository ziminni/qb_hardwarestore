Phase 1b lifecycle — implementation, not deployment

CPMS import → WAITING → store ACCEPTED (every line true/false, at least one true) or whole DECLINED (reason required). Null intent means unanswered, not false. Strict booleans/reasons, no quantity/price/status edits. Accept reserves no stock and books no money. Only accepted lines release; PARTIAL keeps token live, eligible-line completion RELEASED spends it. DECLINED/CLOSED also spend it; typed WAITING/DECLINED scan refusals precede token-used checks. All transitions share token→request locks and fresh state.

Expected amount is known persisted released sale value + current-price true-line outstanding (outstanding sum HALF_UP once), independent of debt/payments. Unknown historical baseline stays null. CLOSED after acceptance retains abandoned shortfall in budget; before review stays null. CPMS read-back adds intent/reasons/budget, no unit prices, stock or token.
